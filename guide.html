🕐 Photo Clock Widget

User Guide — Make your home screen beautiful

📦 Getting Started

1 Install the app from Google Play Store.
2 Long-press on your home screen → tap "Widgets".
3 Find "Photo Clock Widget" → choose a size (2×1, 3×1, 4×1, or 4×2) → drag it to your home screen.
4 The Editor opens automatically. Customize your widget, then tap Save.
The 4×2 widget shows the most information (day + date + time). The 2×1 is the most compact.

🎨 Customizing Your Widget

Background

  • None — transparent, shows your wallpaper
  • Solid — pick any color from the palette
  • Gradient — choose two colors for a smooth gradient
  • Image — pick a photo from your gallery, then pinch-to-zoom to crop

Clock Settings

  • Time Format — 12-hour (10:15 AM) or 24-hour (10:15)
  • Date Format — choose from 9 formats (e.g., "Sun 30 Aug", "30/08/2026")
  • Show Seconds — toggle seconds display
  • Show Date — toggle date line
  • Show Day — toggle day of week

Style

  • Font Size — slider from small to large
  • Text Color — 12 preset colors or pick your own
  • Alignment — left, center, or right

Image Adjustments (when using photo background)

  • Overlay — None / Dark / Light — helps text readability
  • Overlay Opacity — 0% to 70%
  • Blur — blur the background image
  • Text Shadow — adds shadow for better readability

⭐ Presets

Don't want to tweak every setting? Use a preset:

  1. Open the app → tap "Presets"
  2. Browse 8 curated styles: Classic, Modern, Minimal, Bold, Neon, Sunset, Ocean, Mono
  3. Tap one to apply it instantly
Some presets are premium. Watch a short ad to unlock them for the day, or purchase "Remove Ads" to unlock everything permanently.

🖼️ My Designs

Save your favorite combinations as reusable designs:

  1. From the home screen, tap "My Designs"
  2. Tap "+" to create a new design
  3. Customize in the Editor → tap Save
  4. Give it a name → it's saved!

You can save up to 3 designs for free. Long-press a design to Apply, Rename, Delete, or Share.

🔔 Notification Bar Date

Show today's date as a persistent icon in your notification bar:

  1. Open the app → tap "Enable Notification"
  2. Grant notification permission when prompted
  3. The date icon appears in your notification bar
On Android 13+, you need to grant "Notifications" permission. The app will guide you through this.

📱 Floating Bar

A small time/date bar that floats below your status bar, visible across all apps:

  1. Open the app → tap "Enable Floating Bar"
  2. Grant "Display over other apps" permission
  3. The floating bar appears below your status bar
The floating bar does NOT modify your status bar. It's a separate overlay that sits below it.

❓ FAQ

Widget disappeared after reboot?

The widget should persist. If it doesn't, re-add it from the Widgets menu. Your settings are saved automatically.

Widget background is black?

Open the app → Editor → set a background (Solid, Gradient, or Image) → Save. The widget will update within seconds.

Floating bar not showing?

Check that "Display over other apps" permission is granted. On Android 15+, the app must be open at least once after reboot.

How to change widget size?

Long-press the widget on your home screen → drag the resize handles. The widget layout adjusts automatically.

Notification icon not showing date?

The notification icon is a small date indicator. For full date/time, use the widget or floating bar.
